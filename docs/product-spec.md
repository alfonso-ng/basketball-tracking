Include:
- The app is for one person anlaysing recorded basketball games.
- It runs on Windows, MacOS and Linux.
- It works locally without requiring an account or internet connection.
- The user imports videos from their computer into a managed media library.
- The user creates games, teams, rosters and periods.
- The user manually records events while watching a video.
- Events are associated with players, teams, periods, and video timestamps where applicable.
- Statistics are calculated from recorded events.
- The user can correct mistakes and export reports.
- Games and analysis survive closing and reopening the app.

Explicitly exclude from version 1:
- Automatic player or ball tracking.
- AI-generated statistics.
- Live game recording.
- Cloud accounts and synchronisation.
- Multi-user collaboration.
- Advanced tactical tracking and shot-location maps.
